Frequently Asked Questions

How does RadHIL's AI-driven pricing work?

Our AI-driven pricing system analyzes market trends, seasonal demand, local events, and competitor pricing in real-time. It then adjusts your property's rates automatically to maximize occupancy and revenue.

Can I integrate RadHIL with my existing property management software?

Yes, RadHIL offers seamless integration with most popular property management software. Our team can assist you with the integration process to ensure smooth data flow between systems.

How secure is my data on RadHIL's platform?

We take data security very seriously. RadHIL uses bank-level encryption to protect your data, and we comply with all relevant data protection regulations, including GDPR.

What kind of support is available for Enterprise users?

Enterprise users receive 24/7 premium support, including a dedicated account manager, priority email and phone support, and quarterly business reviews to ensure you're getting the most out of RadHIL.

Can RadHIL help me manage guest communication across multiple booking platforms?

Absolutely! Our unified inbox feature centralizes all guest communications from various booking platforms, allowing you to manage all interactions efficiently from a single dashboard.
