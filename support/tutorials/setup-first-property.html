Setting Up Your First Property

Follow this step-by-step guide to add and configure your first property in RadHIL.

Step 1: Access the Property Dashboard

🏠

Log in to your RadHIL account and navigate to the "Properties" section in the main dashboard. Click on the "Add New Property" button to begin the setup process.

Step 2: Enter Basic Property Information

📝

Fill in the basic details of your property, including:

  • Property name
  • Address
  • Property type (e.g., apartment, house, villa)
  • Number of bedrooms and bathrooms
  • Maximum occupancy

Step 3: Upload Property Photos

🖼️

Upload high-quality photos of your property. Make sure to include:

  • Exterior shots
  • Living areas
  • Bedrooms
  • Bathrooms
  • Kitchen
  • Any unique features or amenities

Step 4: Set Up Pricing

💰

Configure your pricing settings:

  • Base nightly rate
  • Seasonal adjustments
  • Weekend/weekday differentiation
  • Special event pricing
  • Minimum stay requirements

Remember, you can always fine-tune these later with our AI pricing optimization tool.

Step 5: Configure Availability Calendar

📅

Set up your property's availability:

  • Mark any dates the property is unavailable
  • Set check-in and check-out days if applicable
  • Configure advance booking window

Step 6: Add Property Amenities and House Rules

📋

List all amenities your property offers and set house rules:

  • Wi-Fi, TV, kitchen appliances, etc.
  • Smoking policy
  • Pet policy
  • Quiet hours
  • Check-in and check-out procedures

Step 7: Connect to Booking Channels

🔗

Link your property to various booking platforms:

  • Airbnb
  • Booking.com
  • Vrbo
  • And more...

RadHIL will automatically sync your calendar and pricing across all connected platforms.

Step 8: Review and Activate

✅

Review all the information you've entered for accuracy. Once you're satisfied, click the "Activate Property" button to make your listing live.

Congratulations! Your property is now set up and ready to receive bookings through RadHIL.
