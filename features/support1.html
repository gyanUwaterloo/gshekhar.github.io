Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password? +
To reset your password, go to the login page and click on "Forgot Password". Follow the instructions sent to your email to create a new password.
Can I integrate RadHIL with my existing property management software? +
Yes, RadHIL offers API integration with many popular property management software solutions. Check our integration page for a list of supported platforms or contact our support team for custom integration options.
How often is the AI pricing algorithm updated? +
Our AI pricing algorithm is continuously learning and updating in real-time. It takes into account market trends, seasonal variations, and your property's unique characteristics to provide optimal pricing recommendations.
Is there a mobile app available for RadHIL? +
Yes, we offer mobile apps for both iOS and Android devices. You can download them from the App Store or Google Play Store to manage your properties on-the-go.
How secure is my data with RadHIL? +
We take data security very seriously. RadHIL uses bank-level encryption, regular security audits, and follows best practices in data protection. For more details, please visit our Security Features page.
