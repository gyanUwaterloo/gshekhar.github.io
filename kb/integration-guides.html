General Integration Tips

  • Always test integrations in a sandbox environment before going live.
  • Regularly review and update your integrations to ensure optimal performance.
  • Make use of RadHIL's integration health monitoring tools to catch issues early.
  • Consider data privacy and security when setting up integrations, especially those involving guest information.
  • Train your team on how to use and troubleshoot integrated systems for smooth operations.
