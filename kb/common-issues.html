Common Issues and Solutions

Welcome to the RadHIL troubleshooting guide. Here, we've compiled a list of common issues that our users encounter and their solutions. If you don't find your specific issue here, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.

1. Calendar Sync Issues

Problem: Bookings are not syncing properly across different platforms.

Solution:

  1. Check your channel connections in the RadHIL dashboard. Ensure all channels are properly connected and authorized.
  2. Verify that the iCal links for each platform are correct and up-to-date.
  3. Try disconnecting and reconnecting the problematic channel.
  4. If the issue persists, perform a manual sync by going to "Channel Management" > "Sync Now".

2. Pricing Algorithm Not Updating

Problem: The AI-driven pricing suggestions are not updating or seem incorrect.

Solution:

  1. Ensure that you've provided accurate base prices and minimum/maximum thresholds for each property.
  2. Check if you have any manual price overrides that might be conflicting with the AI suggestions.
  3. Verify that your market data sources are correctly set up in "Settings" > "Data Sources".
  4. Try running a manual price update by going to "Pricing" > "Update Now".
  5. If issues persist, contact support for a thorough algorithm check.

3. Automated Messages Not Sending

Problem: Guests are not receiving automated messages as configured.

Solution:

  1. Check your automated message settings to ensure they are properly configured and activated.
  2. Verify that the trigger conditions for each message are correct.
  3. Ensure that guest email addresses and phone numbers are correctly entered in the system.
  4. Check your account's messaging credits and limits.
  5. Test the messaging system by sending a manual test message.

4. Performance Analytics Not Loading

Problem: The analytics dashboard is not loading or showing outdated information.

Solution:

  1. Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try reloading the page.
  2. Ensure you're using a supported browser (latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge).
  3. Check your internet connection and try accessing the dashboard from a different network if possible.
  4. Verify that your account has the necessary permissions to view analytics.
  5. If the issue persists, try logging out and logging back in.

5. Integration with Property Management System (PMS) Failing

Problem: RadHIL is not properly syncing with your external PMS.

Solution:

  1. Check the integration settings in both RadHIL and your PMS to ensure they are correctly configured.
  2. Verify that your API keys and access tokens are valid and up-to-date.
  3. Ensure that your PMS is on the list of supported integrations for RadHIL.
  4. Try disconnecting and reconnecting the integration.
  5. Contact both RadHIL support and your PMS support for further assistance if needed.
